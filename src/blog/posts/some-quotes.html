some quotes from books...

02/06/2026 · books

i love reading, i save quotes and never have anywhere to post or anyone to comment them with. i think i am a very lonely person when it comes to having someone to talk to about the books i've read... well, here are my latest quotes:

1. "One good thing about being a woman," she writes, "is we haven't too many examples yet of what a genius looks like. It could be me." (Girl on Girl - Sophie Gilbert)

2. "Beauty isn't actually what you look like; beauty is the preferences that reproduce the existing social order." (Girl on Girl - Sophie Gilbert)

3. "it is fun to devise strategies to 'win the game' of buying a car and commuting to work every day. why? as always, the function of dopamine derives from the imperatives of evolution and survival. it encourages us to maximize our resources by rewarding us when we do so - the act of doing something well, of making our future a better and safer place, gives us a small dopamine 'rush'. (...) when control dopamine is weak, people go after what they want without thinking about long-term consequences." (The Molecule of More - Daniel Z. Lieberman)

4. "dopamine makes promises it is not in a position to keep. 'if you buy those shoes, your life will change', says the desire circuit, and that may happen, but not because of dopamine. (...) therefore, dopamine is not the molecule of pleasure: it is the molecule of anticipation." (The Molecule of More - Daniel Z. Lieberman)

5. "drugs are fundamentally different from natural dopamine triggers. when we are hungry, there is nothing more motivating than getting food. however, after we eat, the motivation to get food decreases because satiety circuits become active and shut down the desire circuit. there are checks and balances to keep the system stable. but there is no satiety circuit for crack." (The Molecule of More - Daniel Z. Lieberman)

6. "'a woman who works', Clara continued, 'especially a woman of our age, always carries the struggle between the traditional woman she was taught to be and the independent one she chose to become. there is a continuous conflict within her.'" (Forbidden Notebook - Alba de Céspedes)

7. "the beauty that cerullo had in her head since she was a child found no way out, greco, and ended up all in her face, her breasts, her thighs, her behind, places where it passes quickly and it is as if it had never existed." (My Brilliant Friend - Elena Ferrante)

8. "within a few decades, i argued, ai systems could replace 'intellectual manual labor' in the same way, certainly long before robots replace physical labor. in the past, new jobs were created at the same time as old ones became obsolete, but what if ai could simply perform most of the new tasks as well?" (The Coming Wave - Mustafa Suleyman)

9. "most men and women express power in ways that had to do with the binary behind everything: for men, it was demonstrations of domination, strength and virility; for women, it was sexuality, pleasure and femininity." (The House of Hidden Meanings - RuPaul Charles)

10. "i know the world is a difficult place and i know that being kind is a choice i can make to ease for another person the anguish of being alive." (The House of Hidden Meanings - RuPaul Charles)
