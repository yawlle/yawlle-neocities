About writing...

22/05/2026 · thinking

my first text could obviously be about my origins on the internet and how my feelings for it changed over these years as it went through transformations, but i think i will talk first about the core of the creation of this blog: writing things.

when i was a child, i participated in many hobbies where the center of the matter was writing. whether playing interpretive rpg, reading and writing fanfics, participating in forums and typing on msn, i always expressed myself through text, and reading texts was always the form of entertainment i liked the most.

but i think i gradually lost the enthusiasm to write (or type xD) texts talking about something, i can't define a specific cause, but i believe i started to feel that i had nothing to say, nor anyone who would read what i wrote. but do i really need someone to read it? someone to respond? i think i spent so much time wondering if there is merit in writing something if there is no one to read or respond to it, that i stopped thinking that the biggest reader of what i could write could be myself. i don't use twitter anymore, but what i liked the most about twitter, besides seeing small daily phrases from my friends, was reading what i myself had written, thoughts i had at that moment, stored like a great book of quotes, available for me to reread and remember the past.

i hope the blog can also help me get back to writing for whoever reads me the most, my biggest fan, myself.
