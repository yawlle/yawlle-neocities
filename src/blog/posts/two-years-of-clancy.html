Two Years of Clancy!

24/05/2026 · music

today is the 2 year anniversary of my favorite twenty one pilots album, and i think it would be more than fair to make a post on the blog talking a little about how the songs touched me in a way no other album from the band managed to. it is very hard for a band or artist you like to release an album that represents a concept that you identify with and enjoy every single song. even concept albums, which involve the same story, have a song or another that you will like less. clancy for me is very much about anxiety and about having dealt for a long time with feelings related to anxiety. as you get older, you manage to better distinguish the triggers, avoid the thoughts that you know will lead you down a crazy path, and try to live a life with better quality, but you are never fully healed, there is no cure, and i think that is what the album is about. i will talk a little about some songs from the album.

next semester
this song got to me quite a bit when it came out, first because it reminded me a lot of the pop-punk style i listened to a lot when i was young with blink 182, green day, etc. besides the nostalgia, what stuck with me the most was the song's lyrics. not only the anxiety of being in academic environments in college, which we already know is a nightmare on its own, but the anxiety of feeling that you don't live in the right place and that you are not living the life you would like to live. "i prayed those lights would take me home" this phrase got to me quite a bit, mainly because when you have a long history with depression and anxiety, being "home" (and what would that home be? your parents' house? your hometown? deep down there is no true home) would be a wonderful rest. but i think despite the song's strong rhythm, there is still hope when it says at the end "start fresh with the new year". i always believed that the next year would be better.

backslide
even though i don't like the melody of backslide, the lyrics "i don't wanna backslide to where i've started from, there's no chance i will shake this again" remind me a lot of this feeling that every fall and breakdown we have is a failure or a return to previous episodes... midwest índigo i really like the phrase "and i want love and sunny days, i'm a bit too old to run away", mainly because i heard that tyler changed "i'm a bit too old to feel this way" to "to run away", which makes much more sense, because it is not age that will stop us from feeling certain old feelings again... but from running away, that it will.

routines in the night
i have never been one to have insomnia, my episodes of anxiety and depression affect my sleep by making me sleep too much, so it is curious to identify with this song, more in the sense of the mystery and the crowd of thoughts that occur inside my head throughout the day. i really like this music video, there are some movements from tyler that look a lot like the ones damon albarn does in the universal music video xD

vignette
this song is really good, the instrumental, the lyrics, everything in it is very good and tyler's little hands when he says "flesh covered in bites"

lavish
WELCOMETONEWWAYY ISS JUST THE BEGINNIIING OF LAVISHHH what lavish brought good to my life is that every time me and my boyfriend go to a fancy place to have a fancy experience, we sing lavish.


navigating
is an important chapter for the lore, as we better understand the role of josh/torchbearer within the story. i also think it is an important song to support the theory about the clancybearer couple, of which i am a big fan and supporter. "but when our fingers touch, i feel my way back home"? please, that is pure cinema.

snapback
definitely one of the songs i like the most from the album, and the one that best describes my feelings about anxiety and depression (mainly because the song coincidentally talks about 2017, which was a year i had a very strong depressive peak xD). i really like how well the song describes that exhausting moment when you think "yeah, i held on for quite a while", how much that can bring consequences for the people who live around us at the moment we say "well, it was good while it lasted, it's a backslide, i hate the surprise, i have seemed to run out of excuses of why i'm this way". i always feel that all these feelings that consume and chew my brain are one step away from appearing, always lurking there, waiting for something to happen in my personal life, at work, or even in the world, to take over me and make me doubt my will to remain in this world.

oldies station
i understand the feeling of this song for fans, mainly about getting older, about the things we learn as we get older, about how bizarre it is that "your favorite song was on the oldies station" (it is not a phrase i identify with, mainly because most of the songs i listen to were already on the oldies station before i was even born xD, i know fans also connect because stressed out for example is already inside these oldies stations, which was not my case, because i started to appreciate the song almost 5 to 10 years after it was released), but in the end i don't identify that much with this song, mainly because i have no anxiety about getting older and looking at my own past with fond eyes.

at the risk of feeling dumb
this song touches me quite a bit, because i have been on both sides of the song: the friend who refuses help and attention because they don't want to be a bother and also the friend who worries and wants to check if their friend is okay. i lost a great friend of mine to s**cide in 2020 and that marked me forever, both for the absence i feel from this friend, and for the traumatic experience itself of receiving a goodbye message on my cellphone. but it is complicated, i understand, in bad moments i prefer to isolate myself and try to get through everything alone, i don't want my friends to have the lyrics of the outsider - a perfect circle going through their heads when it say "if you choose to pull the trigger, should your drama prove sincere, do it somewhere far away from here".

paladin strait
this song is beautiful, a very beautiful closing for the album, a very important step for the lore, which is the beginning of his battle against blurryface and the other bishops. this ending "hello, clancy" was an absurd plot twist for us when we heard it in 2024.

i think that is it! the album came out at a time when i really needed changes in my life, to prove myself, a need to fulfill my dreams and be part of something bigger, but also to heal and feel better about myself. happy 2 years, clancy!
