ABOUT ME!

hi everyone, i'm yawlle. i am in my 20s, i live in brazil and i am a programmer. i really like doing little artistic things like painting, drawing and creating. i am not very good at them, in the past i was very fixated on hobbies that involved computers, but i wanted to get away from the screens and ventured into doing more little manual things!

i have always really liked computers. i remember that me and my family got one when i was around 6 years old, and since then, this incredible machine full of possibilities exists for me. more than computers, i think i really like the internet: talking to people far away, creating pages with shiny images that move, everything was always so incredible! i think my coolest childhood moments are probably me sitting in a chair, facing a little screen, with the afternoon passing slowly outside...

but then time passed, and with it came all this transformation that the internet went through, of everything being much less about community, but about money, getting more addiction, an absurd battle for your attention in every site, in every app you open... anyway! emotional subject for me!

games

i don't play a wide variety of games, and i have been playing very little lately. the titles i enjoyed the most: club penguin, papers please, transformice, gta v, red dead redemption 2, cyberpunk 2077, gris, stardew valley, cult of the lamb, no man's sky, stray, sable, i can't remember more xD
i played league of legends for a long time, but i believe the game brings me feelings that are definitely not worth feeling... i haven't played since 2022.

books

i really like books, i have been very interested in biographies, non-fiction, with a focus on reports, chronicles, testimonial literature and literary journalism. i had a phase of really liking horror and suspense books, but i have been reading little of them lately. i also really like feminist literature.
i want to go deeper into the books i read on my blog...

music

i really like music, i should definitely write more about music on my blog. i think i have liked music for as long as i can remember. my parents always liked to collect cds and listen to music loud at home. we used to sit on the couch at the end of sunday and watch dvds with lots of music videos, from many bands, and comment on our favorite ones together. i love watching music videos, if songs are already great, would mini movies of those songs not be even more incredible???? my favorite music styles are classic rock, darkwave, goth, synthpop, eurodance, punk and some emo bands from 2000's.
